Braised Leeks

March 3, 2019

Serves 4

DIRECTIONS

Trim the leeks, halve them lengthwise and rinse out any grit under cold running water.

Lay them cut side down in a wide pan with the butter and stock, cover, and simmer gently for 25 minutes.

Uncover, raise the heat and reduce the liquid to a glaze, then season with salt and pepper.

Print This Recipe

INGREDIENTS

4 large leeks

2 tablespoons butter

1 cup chicken stock

Fine sea salt
