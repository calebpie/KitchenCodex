Weeknight Lasagne

Steps

1

Making the Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 onion
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup crushed tomatoes
Brown the garlic, onion and beef, then simmer with the tomatoes until thick.
2

Making the White Sauce

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups whole milk
Make a roux with the butter and flour, then whisk in the milk until smooth.
3

Assembling

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 8 oz mozzarella
Layer noodles, sauce, white sauce and cheese, then bake until bubbling.
