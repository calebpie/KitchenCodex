Grilled Flank Steak Recipe

May 14, 2015

Servings: 4-6
Prep time: 15 minutes
Total time: 1 hour, plus marinating

This is the steak we make all summer, and it is the reason we bought a grill in the first place. You will need:

for the marinade:
½ cup|120 ml soy sauce
¼ cup|60 ml lime juice
3 garlic cloves, grated
2 pounds|900 grams flank steak, trimmed of any silverskin and patted completely dry before it goes into the marinade

to serve:
warm tortillas
lime wedges
flaky salt

Whisk the soy sauce, lime juice and garlic with ¼ cup|60 ml water and pour over the steak; refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

Grill over high heat until the steak reaches 125°F for medium-rare, about 5 minutes a side.

Rest for 10 minutes, slice thinly against the grain, and serve with the tortillas and lime.
