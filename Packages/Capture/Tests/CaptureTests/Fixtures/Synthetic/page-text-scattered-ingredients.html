My Grandmother's Coconut Rice

My grandmother never wrote this down, so these are the amounts I measured while watching her make it one Sunday.

1 cup jasmine rice

She rinsed the rice three times in a bowl, swirling it with her fingers until the water ran almost clear.

1 1/4 cups coconut milk

The coconut milk went into the pot first, with a little water, and she brought it just to a simmer before anything else.

1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

Then the rice and salt went in, the lid went on, and the heat went down as low as it would go for eighteen minutes.

Let it rest off the heat for ten minutes before fluffing it with a fork.
