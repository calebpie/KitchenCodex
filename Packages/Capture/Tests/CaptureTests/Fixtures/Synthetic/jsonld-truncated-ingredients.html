Herbed Rice Salad

Serves 6 | Level: Easy

From the book:

Pantry Notes Weekday!

  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain rice
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 cup chopped parsley
  • For the Dressing:
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • To Assemble:
  • 3 scallions, minced
  • 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta

Cook the rice and spread it out to cool.

Whisk the dressing.
