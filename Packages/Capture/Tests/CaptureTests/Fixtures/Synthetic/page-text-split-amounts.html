Oven

Take the salmon out of the fridge thirty minutes ahead so it roasts evenly all the way through.

15 g

Kosher salt

Salt the salmon on both sides and let it sit while the oven heats to 275 °F.

Rub with the oil and roast until the center reaches 120 °F, about 25 minutes.

Tip
Use a probe thermometer.

Scatter the lemon zest and dill over the fish and serve right away.
