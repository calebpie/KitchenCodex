Simple Pancakes

By Lee Sample

Serves 4, ready in 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg

Method

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together.
  2. Beat in the milk and egg until just combined.
  3. Cook on a hot griddle until bubbles form, then flip.
