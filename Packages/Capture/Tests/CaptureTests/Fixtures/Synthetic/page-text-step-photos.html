Roast Duck

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

1

Pekin duckwhole

35 g

Kosher salt

divided

100 g

Hoisin sauce

2 L

Watercold

Pat the duck dry inside and out and leave it uncovered in the fridge overnight.

Rub the cavity with the salt and hoisin and truss the legs together with twine.

Roast at 350 °F until the skin is deep amber and the thigh reads 165 °F.

Carve the skin off first, then the breast, and serve with pancakes and scallions.
