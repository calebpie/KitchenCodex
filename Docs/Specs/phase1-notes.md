So 1/4 cup × 1.5 is 6 tbsp, 2 tsp × 1.5 is 1 tbsp, 2 tbsp ÷ 4 is 1 1/2 tsp, 9 tbsp stays 9 tbsp (1/2 cup would be 11% short), 1/3 cup × 1 1/4 is 6 1/2 tbsp, 1 kg is 2 1/4 lb. Counts round to halves, with quarters and thirds below one and whole numbers from ten; pinches and dashes to whole numbers; "to taste" is untouched. Rounding is idempotent and never turns a nonzero amount into zero. `formatted(style:)` shows Unicode vulgar fractions for US units and counts ("1½ cups", "⅓ cup", "7/16 cup" when there is no glyph), decimals for metric ("1.5 kg", "235 mL"), plural names above one, and `.full` spells units out. `UnitParser` maps spellings ("Tbsp", "fl. oz.", "lbs", cookbook T/t) to `CookingUnit`. `MeasurementSystem.dominant(in:)` picks a recipe's system, treating spoons, counts and informal units as neutral; `CookingUnit.system` is `.us` for spoons because the conversion tables need it. |

### Decisions made in this slice

- **No schema change.** Schema v1 had every field this slice needed; `SchemaTests.followsCloudKitRules` still passes untouched.
- **Cover photo storage.** The picked photo is re-encoded as JPEG (longest side 2,400 px) and stored as a `RecipeImage` with role `.photo` and caption "Cover" (external storage). A 900 px copy goes in `Recipe.coverImageData` (also external storage) so cards and rows don't decode the full photo; the detail hero uses the full one. Replacing or removing the cover deletes the old `.photo` image; page scans and cook photos are never touched.
- **When an edit makes a version.** Only changes to what lives on `RecipeVersion` (servings, yield, times, ingredients, steps, notes) add a version. Title, course and photo live on `Recipe` and change in place. Change summary defaults to "Added by hand" and "Edited".
- **Ingredient text round trip.** The editor shows each ingredient's `rawText`, so an edit re-parses exactly what was typed; steps are joined with blank lines so a multi-line step survives.
- **Unit toggle.** The detail shows amounts as written until the cook taps US or Metric: no cross-system conversion, and when the servings are scaled the unit is re-picked only within the ingredient's own system (so 1/4 cup × 1.5 still reads 6 tbsp before any tap); the control starts on the recipe's dominant system. `IngredientLine` is `scaled(by:)`, then `displayed(in:)` when a system applies, then `friendlyRounded().formatted()`. The choice is per visit, not a stored preference (the Profile pane's `UserProfile.unitPreference` is for later in Phase 1).
- **One Units API.** Slice 1's `FriendlyQuantity.swift` (`Double`-based `Quantity.friendly(scaledBy:in:)`) was folded into PR #1's exact-rational design on Oct 17, 2026, keeping its cook-facing behaviours (spoons stay spoons in metric, the 5% neighbouring-unit rule, `dominant(in:)`). Where the two disagreed the cook's measure won: pints are shown as cups (1 1/2 pints reads 3 cups); 1 cup in metric is 235 mL, not 240 (the 5 mL grid below 300); 1 1/2 pinches reads 2 pinches; `formatted()` uses vulgar fractions and "mL"; tablespoons written as "4 tbsp" read 1/4 cup once US is tapped, because the display step prefers the largest clean unit.
- **More menu placement.** Recipes' More menu moved from `.secondaryAction` to `.primaryAction`: on iOS 27 the secondary placement nests it inside the system overflow menu, so More opened a menu that held only "More".

### Known limitations to revisit

- Ranges keep only the low end ("2-3 cloves" scales from 2); the raw text keeps the rest. Showing a scaled range needs an upper-bound field on `Ingredient` (a schema change, so not done here).
- No volume ↔ mass conversion (cups of flour to grams) until ingredient densities exist; flour in metric shows milliliters.
- Count items aren't pluralized ("1 1/2 large egg"), and sizes stay in the item text ("large eggs").
- Title, course and photo edits are not versioned (see decisions above).

### Import paths and confidence

| Path | When | Confidence | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| JSON-LD | Any `Recipe` node in any ld+json block: bare, in arrays, in `@graph`, under `mainEntity`; `@id` references resolved for authors, publishers and images. Several recipes: the one whose name matches the page title, else the first. | 0.95 | no |
| JSON-LD, page list | The page text holds a clearly longer ingredient list (at least 3 more lines and 1.5×) containing most of the structured lines: the page's list is used (Barefoot Contessa's JSON-LD lists only its last group). | 0.85 | no |
| JSON-LD, page steps | No instructions in the structured data; steps come from the page. | 0.75 | no |
| Microdata | `itemprop="recipeIngredient"` (or `ingredients`) inside a `schema.org/Recipe` scope, at least 2 lines. | 0.85 | no |
| Page text | The densest run of lines `IngredientParser` reads as ingredients, with a bonus under an "Ingredients" heading; group headings and short unparsed lines ("Kosher salt") allowed inside. Amounts split into their own elements are rejoined ("6.5 g" + "Szechuan pepper" + "whole, divided"). Steps: under a Directions/Method/Instructions heading after the list, else a numbered sequence (titles joined to their paragraphs, per-step ingredient lists skipped), else such a heading above the list, else the paragraphs after it. | Model | The deterministic result is missing or below 0.7 and the on-device model is available. Its reading replaces the page-text one only if it keeps at least 80% of the ingredient lines; group headings carry over by line. | at most 0.6 | yes |

### Decisions made in this slice

- **One save path for all three entries.** `RecipeDraft.swift`, `ImportedRecipe+Draft.swift`, `ImportSummaryView.swift`, `LibraryOptions.swift` and `RecipeCoverImage.swift` (for `ImageProcessing`) are compiled into the Share Extension through a membership exception on the `RecipeCodex` synchronized folder. The extension target also gained the Units and Intelligence products and the app's `SWIFT_DEFAULT_ACTOR_ISOLATION = MainActor`. No capability, entitlement or signing change.
- **Editor components shared.** `NumberField`, `PlaceholderTextEditor` and `ParsedIngredientRow` in `RecipeEditorView.swift` went from `private` to internal so the import preview reuses them; nothing else in that file changed.
- **Safari's JavaScript result.** Safari registers it only as `com.apple.property-list`, and `loadDataRepresentation` returns nothing for it; `loadItem(forTypeIdentifier:)` is the only reader and is deprecated in iOS 27. It is called through a one-method protocol so the build has no warnings.
- **`captureConfidence` is set for web imports.** RecipeModel's doc comment says it is nil for web import; with import confidence now meaningful, that comment is stale (RecipeModel was out of scope here).
- **Servings ranges** read as the low end with the wording kept: "Servings: 4-6" saves 4 servings and the yield "Serves 4–6".
- **Shared recipes appear in the running app.** A recipe saved by the extension showed up in Recipes without relaunching the app (same process before and after), so no history observer was needed for this.

**How (a) decides.** Only when the structured data has no groups and at least 4 lines: the page's lines are walked in order, and a structured line counts as found when the page shows the same line (normalized) no more than two lines ahead of the last match. A heading (a heading element or a group-heading line, not "Ingredients", not the recipe's title, at most 50 characters) names the next found line when it sits at most three lines above it. It applies when at least 80% of the lines are found and at least two different groups result. **(b)** strips "Yield", "Yields", "Servings", "Portions" with or without a separator ("YIELD —", "Yield:", "Servings 4"), and only the separator after "Serves" and "Makes" ("Serves: 4" → "Serves 4"). **(c)** moves a step that starts with "Do ahead" or "Make ahead" (any case, hyphen or space), "Note:", "Notes:", "Tip:", "Tips:", "Cook's note:" (with a colon or dash), or "NOTE"/"TIP" in capitals, to the end of the notes in order; "Tip the batter into the pan" stays a step, and a recipe whose only steps are asides keeps them. It applies to every path, including Paprika and Mela. **(d)** `HTMLPage` now records where sibling inline elements meet within a line; after a lone amount, a following line made of a capitalized name and a short lowercase last element is rejoined as "amount name, note". Steps are never split or merged.

### Decisions made in this slice

- **Schema.** One field added to schema v1 in place: `UserProfile.temperaturePreference`, an enum with a default, so `SchemaTests.followsCloudKitRules` holds. The existing simulator stores opened without a migration.
- **What the units preference does.** "Amounts written in the other system convert to yours" rather than "every amount is redrawn in your system": a US recipe keeps its tablespoons (slice 1's "4 tbsp reads ¼ cup once US is tapped" would otherwise hit every recipe), and spoons stay spoons in a metric preference. A metric recipe under the US preference does convert: ChefSteps' 6.5 g Szechuan pepper reads ¼ oz, 180 g soy sauce 6¼ oz. Tapping Metric shows the grams for that visit.
- **Which ingredients "need a check".** No amount (to taste excepted) or no item: the low-confidence signal 03 §3.6 names for scans ("lines that did not parse into quantity and item"). Serving lines with no amount ("cooked rice", "ssamjang") are counted; the cook clears them in one tap.
- **Clearing review.** Save in review mode clears `needsReview` even with no changes, and Looks Right clears it from detail; neither adds a version. An edit that changes content adds a version as before, and new versions start unflagged.
- **Smart collections only.** Schema v1 has no collection model, so the sidebar's Collections are Favorites, Never Cooked and Needs a Look; user-made collections need a schema change or tags of a collection kind.
- **Rendering only on a miss.** The web view runs only when the fetched HTML yields no recipe, so JSON-LD sites stay one fetch. `FetchedPage`'s initializer is internal to Capture, so the loader returns its own `RenderedPage` and calls `WebRecipeImporter.extract(html:url:)`; Capture is unchanged.
- **Cook's review queue uses view destinations.** A value `NavigationLink` inside a page that a view link pushed stalled the push (the tab bar hid, nothing appeared).
- **Spotlight description.** Putting the ingredient list into `contentDescription` did not make ingredient-only queries find the recipe either, so the description stays source · time · course.
