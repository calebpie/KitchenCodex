## What this slice covers

| Area | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Recipes view | `RecipeCodex/Tabs/RecipesView.swift` | Library from SwiftData as a two-column card grid (adaptive, so more columns on iPad) or a list. More (⋯) holds View As (Grid/List), Sort By (Title, Recently Added) and a disabled Select; both choices persist in `@AppStorage`. Filter chips under the Recipes · Books switch: **Course** (standard courses plus any found in the library) and **Cooked** (Any, Never Cooked, Cooked); an active chip is tinted and names its value, with a clear button beside it. Swipe right in the list to favorite. Long-press: Cook (disabled until Phase 3), Favorite/Unfavorite, Delete, which confirms first. Empty library keeps the Phase 0 empty state; a filter that matches nothing shows "No matching recipes" with Clear Filters. Books stays an empty state. |

Row: 56 pt cover, title, course, time, badge. Covers decode off the main actor with ImageIO at display size and are cached; recipes without a photo get a quiet New York monogram (03 §6). |
| Add menu | `RecipesView.swift` | Add (+) is enabled; **New Recipe** opens the editor. Import from Web, Scan Page and Add Book stay disabled. |
| Manual entry and edit | `RecipeCodex/Library/RecipeEditorView.swift`, `RecipeDraft.swift` | One form for New Recipe and Edit: cover photo (Photos picker, change or remove), title, course, servings, yield text, prep/cook/total minutes, ingredients (one per line, with a live "Reads As" preview of how each line parsed), steps (one per paragraph), notes, and on edit an optional "What changed?" summary. Cancel with changes asks before discarding; swipe-to-dismiss is blocked while there are changes. Save is disabled until there is a title (new) or a change (edit). |
| Recipe detail | `RecipeCodex/Library/RecipeDetailView.swift` | Hero photo under the navigation bar with a soft scroll edge effect; the inline title appears once the large title scrolls away. Title, source line ("Added by hand · date", or book and page, or site and author), time, yield, course, cooked state. Servings stepper rescales every amount live; "Scaled from 4" shows under it. Recipes without servings get a batch multiplier (× 1/4 … × 4) instead. US · Metric segmented control. Ingredients with the amount in bold, numbered steps, notes, and a Versions disclosure listing every version with its date and summary, current one marked. Edit (toolbar) opens the editor prefilled; More holds Favorite and Delete (confirms, deletes after the pop). Cook is the prominent bottom-bar button, disabled until Phase 3; the tab bar hides on this screen so the bottom bar shows. |
| Search tab | `RecipeCodex/Tabs/SearchView.swift` | Title and ingredient search over the library. Every word must match the title or an ingredient of the current version (diacritic- and case-insensitive); results are grouped under **Recipes**, title matches first, and an ingredient match shows "Uses kosher salt" under the title. Opens the same detail view. |
| Ingredient parser | `Packages/Capture/Sources/Capture/IngredientParser.swift` | Deterministic `IngredientParser.parse(_:)` / `parseLines(_:)` → `ParsedIngredient` (quantity as `Rational`, `CookingUnit`, item, preparation, optional flag, raw text). Handles whole numbers, fractions, mixed numbers, decimals, Unicode fractions ("1½"), "1-1/2", ranges ("2-3", "2 to 3"; keeps the low end), attached units ("500g"), abbreviations with periods, cookbook T/t, "fl oz", "of", "a pinch", "(optional)", "to taste", and a parenthetical equivalent measure ("1 cup (240 ml) milk" drops the "(240 ml)"; "1 (14 oz) can" keeps it). Capture now depends on Units. |
| Step splitter | `Packages/Capture/Sources/Capture/StepSplitter.swift` | Paragraphs become steps when the text has blank lines (wrapped lines inside a paragraph are joined); otherwise each line is a step. Typed numbering ("1.", "2)", "Step 3:") is dropped, but "1.5 hours later" is not. |
| Scaling and rounding | `Packages/Units/Sources/Units/FriendlyQuantity.swift` | `Quantity.friendly(scaledBy:in:)`: scales exactly with `Rational`, converts when a system is given, then rounds to something measurable. Cups use quarters and thirds, teaspoons quarters, tablespoons halves, ounces and pounds quarters; if the amount doesn't land within 5% it tries the neighbouring unit (1/4 cup × 1.5 is 6 tbsp, 2 tsp × 1.5 is 1 tbsp, 2 tbsp ÷ 4 is 1 1/2 tsp), then finer fractions (3/4 cup × 1.5 is 1 1/8 cups). Counts round to halves (quarters and thirds below one). Metric rounds to 0.5 g below 10, 1 g below 50, 5 g below 100, 10 g above, and switches to kg/L at 1,000 (1 cup is 240 ml, 1 lb is 450 g); scaled metric amounts that are already multiples of 5 stay exact (250 g × 1.5 is 375 g). Teaspoons and tablespoons stay spoons in metric up to half a cup. `formatted()` shows mixed numbers for US units and counts ("1 1/2 cups"), decimals for metric ("1.5 kg"), plural units above one. `MeasurementSystem.dominant(in:)` picks a recipe's system. |
| Version helpers | `Packages/RecipeModel/Sources/RecipeModel/Library+Versions.swift` | Computed conveniences only: `sortedVersions`, `nextVersionNumber`, `addVersion(_:)` (numbers it, appends it, makes it current, touches `updatedAt`), `coverImage`, `cookCount`, `isNeverCooked`, `sortedIngredients`, `sortedSteps`, `displayMinutes`. |

### Decisions made in this slice

- **No schema change.** Schema v1 had every field this slice needed; `SchemaTests.followsCloudKitRules` still passes untouched.
- **Cover photo storage.** The picked photo is re-encoded as JPEG (longest side 2,400 px) and stored as a `RecipeImage` with role `.photo` and caption "Cover" (external storage). A 900 px copy goes in `Recipe.coverImageData` (also external storage) so cards and rows don't decode the full photo; the detail hero uses the full one. Replacing or removing the cover deletes the old `.photo` image; page scans and cook photos are never touched.
- **When an edit makes a version.** Only changes to what lives on `RecipeVersion` (servings, yield, times, ingredients, steps, notes) add a version. Title, course and photo live on `Recipe` and change in place. Change summary defaults to "Added by hand" and "Edited".
- **Ingredient text round trip.** The editor shows each ingredient's `rawText`, so an edit re-parses exactly what was typed; steps are joined with blank lines so a multi-line step survives.
- **Unit toggle.** The detail shows amounts as written until the cook taps US or Metric; the control starts on the recipe's dominant system. The choice is per visit, not a stored preference (the Profile pane's `UserProfile.unitPreference` is for later in Phase 1).
- **More menu placement.** Recipes' More menu moved from `.secondaryAction` to `.primaryAction`: on iOS 27 the secondary placement nests it inside the system overflow menu, so More opened a menu that held only "More".

## Verified

- `swift test`: **Capture** 12 tests (the parser runs 29 parameterized lines including the four from the brief, plus to-taste-with-amount, raw text, blank lines, and "no unit without an amount"; 3 step-splitter tests; the 4 existing JSON-LD tests). **Units** 18 tests (17 four-to-six cases, 6 four-to-one, awkward scales, exactness, conversions both ways, dominant system, formatting). **RecipeModel** 9 tests (existing 5, CloudKit rules included, plus 4 versioning tests: an edit adds version 2, makes it current, and leaves version 1 as history with `isCurrentOf == nil`).
- `xcodebuild build` for the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 27.0): **BUILD SUCCEEDED** with no warnings.
- On the iPhone 17 Pro simulator, by hand: added "Buttermilk Biscuits" with a photo from the Photos picker (a 20 MB HEIC, downsampled fine), 4 servings, times, eight ingredients and three steps; the "Reads As" preview parsed every line. It showed in the grid (photo, "30 min", "Never cooked") and the list. Opened it: hero under the bar, scaled 4 → 6 and saw 3 cups flour, 1 1/2 tbsp baking powder, 2 1/4 tsp kosher salt, 3/4 tsp baking soda, 9 tbsp butter, 1 1/8 cups buttermilk, 1 1/2 large egg, "flaky salt, to taste"; Metric gave 710 ml, 130 ml, 270 ml with the spoons unchanged. Edited course and yield: Versions (2) lists version 2 (current, "Edited") over version 1 ("Added by hand"). Search "kosher" found it under Recipes with "Uses kosher salt". Swipe-to-favorite, the Cooked chip (no-match state and Clear Filters), More → List, and long-press → Delete with its confirmation all worked; deleting the last recipe brought back the original empty state. Data persisted across relaunches.
- Fixed during that run: the More menu nesting (above); the large title disappearing when a filter emptied the list (the grid/list now stays in place with the message as an overlay); 9 tbsp rounding to a half cup that was 11% short (the original unit is now always a fallback candidate); the Cook button hidden behind the tab bar; number fields that only focused when the text itself was tapped.

### Not verified

- The Cook button's title after switching it to `.titleAndIcon` (built, not looked at again); delete from the detail screen's More menu; Recently Added sort on screen (one recipe only).
- Dynamic Type at accessibility sizes, VoiceOver, dark mode, iPad layout, and anything on a device. CloudKit sync of the new records between devices (phase0-notes still lists the sync proof as Caleb's).

## Left for the rest of Phase 1

- **Capture paths:** Share Extension import through `JSONLDRecipeParser` and `IngredientParser`, with the on-device model fallback and the import preview sheet (03 §3.4); in-app browser import; **Paprika and Mela import** (More menu item not added yet).

- **Exports:** PDF, plain text and Markdown.
- **iPad:** three-column `NavigationSplitView` for Recipes, sidebar sections, keyboard shortcuts (⌘N new recipe, ⌘F search), drag and drop into collections and photos onto a recipe.
- **Pantry → Profile pane** with the units preference (`UserProfile.unitPreference`), proficiency and intelligence settings; the detail's unit toggle should start from it.
- **Spotlight** indexing of recipes.

### Known limitations to revisit

- Ranges keep only the low end ("2-3 cloves" scales from 2); the raw text keeps the rest. Showing a scaled range needs an upper-bound field on `Ingredient` (a schema change, so not done here).
- No volume ↔ mass conversion (cups of flour to grams) until ingredient densities exist; flour in metric shows milliliters.
- Count items aren't pluralized ("1 1/2 large egg"), and sizes stay in the item text ("large eggs").
- Title, course and photo edits are not versioned (see decisions above).
