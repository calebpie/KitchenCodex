| Scaling and rounding | `Packages/Units/Sources/Units/` (`UnitConversion.swift`, `FriendlyRounding.swift`, `QuantityFormatting.swift`, `RationalArithmetic.swift`, `UnitParser.swift`) | One pipeline, exact until the last step: `quantity.scaled(by:)` multiplies the `Rational`; `.displayed(in:)` re-expresses it in the unit of the chosen system that reads best (exact conversion tables, 1 cup = 236.588 mL and 1 oz = 28.3495 g held as rationals); `.friendlyRounded()` snaps to what a cook can measure; `.formatted()` prints it. `displayed(in:)` walks the system's units from largest to smallest and takes the first whose value sits on, or within 5% of, its clean grid (cups in quarters or thirds, tablespoons in halves up to 16, whole teaspoons up to 8, pounds in quarters from 1 lb); otherwise the largest unit that fits. So 1/4 cup × 1.5 is 6 tbsp, 2 tsp × 1.5 is 1 tbsp, 2 tbsp ÷ 4 is 1 1/2 tsp, 9 tbsp stays 9 tbsp (1/2 cup would be 11% short), 1/3 cup × 1 1/4 is 6 1/2 tbsp, 1 kg is 2 1/4 lb. Teaspoons and tablespoons stay spoons in metric up to half a cup (8 tbsp), then become millilitres. `friendlyRounded()` tries the marks on the measuring set first (cups: halves, thirds, quarters; teaspoons: halves, quarters; tablespoons: halves; larger US units, ounces and pounds: halves, quarters) and accepts one within 5%; only then a finer fraction (thirds and eighths for spoons, eighths for cups, so 3/4 cup × 1.5 is 1 1/8 cups and 1 tsp ÷ 3 is 1/3 tsp), and a fine fraction within 5% of a mark becomes the mark (2 7/8 tbsp reads 3 tbsp). Grams and millilitres round to 1 below 30, 5 below 300, 10 above, but an amount already on the 5 g grid stays (250 g × 1.5 is 375 g); litres and kilograms to 0.05 (1 cup is 235 mL, 1 lb is 450 g, 3 lb is 1.35 kg). Counts round to halves, with quarters and thirds below one and whole numbers from ten; pinches and dashes to whole numbers; "to taste" is untouched. Rounding is idempotent and never turns a nonzero amount into zero. `formatted(style:)` shows Unicode vulgar fractions for US units and counts ("1½ cups", "⅓ cup", "7/16 cup" when there is no glyph), decimals for metric ("1.5 kg", "235 mL"), plural names above one, and `.full` spells units out. `UnitParser` maps spellings ("Tbsp", "fl. oz.", "lbs", cookbook T/t) to `CookingUnit`. `MeasurementSystem.dominant(in:)` picks a recipe's system, treating spoons, counts and informal units as neutral; `CookingUnit.system` is `.us` for spoons because the conversion tables need it. |
| Version helpers | `Packages/RecipeModel/Sources/RecipeModel/Library+Versions.swift` | Computed conveniences only: `sortedVersions`, `nextVersionNumber`, `addVersion(_:)` (numbers it, appends it, makes it current, touches `updatedAt`), `coverImage`, `cookCount`, `isNeverCooked`, `sortedIngredients`, `sortedSteps`, `displayMinutes`. |

### Decisions made in this slice

- **No schema change.** Schema v1 had every field this slice needed; `SchemaTests.followsCloudKitRules` still passes untouched.
- **Cover photo storage.** The picked photo is re-encoded as JPEG (longest side 2,400 px) and stored as a `RecipeImage` with role `.photo` and caption "Cover" (external storage). A 900 px copy goes in `Recipe.coverImageData` (also external storage) so cards and rows don't decode the full photo; the detail hero uses the full one. Replacing or removing the cover deletes the old `.photo` image; page scans and cook photos are never touched.
- **When an edit makes a version.** Only changes to what lives on `RecipeVersion` (servings, yield, times, ingredients, steps, notes) add a version. Title, course and photo live on `Recipe` and change in place. Change summary defaults to "Added by hand" and "Edited".
- **Ingredient text round trip.** The editor shows each ingredient's `rawText`, so an edit re-parses exactly what was typed; steps are joined with blank lines so a multi-line step survives.
- **Unit toggle.** The detail shows amounts as written until the cook taps US or Metric: no cross-system conversion, and when the servings are scaled the unit is re-picked only within the ingredient's own system (so 1/4 cup × 1.5 still reads 6 tbsp before any tap); the control starts on the recipe's dominant system. `IngredientLine` is `scaled(by:)`, then `displayed(in:)` when a system applies, then `friendlyRounded().formatted()`. The choice is per visit, not a stored preference (the Profile pane's `UserProfile.unitPreference` is for later in Phase 1).
- **One Units API.** Slice 1's `FriendlyQuantity.swift` (`Double`-based `Quantity.friendly(scaledBy:in:)`) was folded into PR #1's exact-rational design on Oct 17, 2026, keeping its cook-facing behaviours (spoons stay spoons in metric, the 5% neighbouring-unit rule, `dominant(in:)`). Where the two disagreed the cook's measure won: pints are shown as cups (1 1/2 pints reads 3 cups); 1 cup in metric is 235 mL, not 240 (the 5 mL grid below 300); 1 1/2 pinches reads 2 pinches; `formatted()` uses vulgar fractions and "mL"; tablespoons written as "4 tbsp" read 1/4 cup once US is tapped, because the display step prefers the largest clean unit.
- **More menu placement.** Recipes' More menu moved from `.secondaryAction` to `.primaryAction`: on iOS 27 the secondary placement nests it inside the system overflow menu, so More opened a menu that held only "More".

## Verified

- `swift test`: **Capture** 12 tests (the parser runs 29 parameterized lines including the four from the brief, plus to-taste-with-amount, raw text, blank lines, and "no unit without an amount"; 3 step-splitter tests; the 4 existing JSON-LD tests). **Units** 18 tests (17 four-to-six cases, 6 four-to-one, awkward scales, exactness, conversions both ways, dominant system, formatting). **RecipeModel** 9 tests (existing 5, CloudKit rules included, plus 4 versioning tests: an edit adds version 2, makes it current, and leaves version 1 as history with `isCurrentOf == nil`).
- `xcodebuild build` for the iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 27.0): **BUILD SUCCEEDED** with no warnings.
- On the iPhone 17 Pro simulator, by hand: added "Buttermilk Biscuits" with a photo from the Photos picker (a 20 MB HEIC, downsampled fine), 4 servings, times, eight ingredients and three steps; the "Reads As" preview parsed every line. It showed in the grid (photo, "30 min", "Never cooked") and the list. Opened it: hero under the bar, scaled 4 → 6 and saw 3 cups flour, 1 1/2 tbsp baking powder, 2 1/4 tsp kosher salt, 3/4 tsp baking soda, 9 tbsp butter, 1 1/8 cups buttermilk, 1 1/2 large egg, "flaky salt, to taste"; Metric gave 710 ml, 130 ml, 270 ml with the spoons unchanged. Edited course and yield: Versions (2) lists version 2 (current, "Edited") over version 1 ("Added by hand"). Search "kosher" found it under Recipes with "Uses kosher salt". Swipe-to-favorite, the Cooked chip (no-match state and Clear Filters), More → List, and long-press → Delete with its confirmation all worked; deleting the last recipe brought back the original empty state. Data persisted across relaunches.
- After folding the two Units implementations together (Oct 17, 2026, later the same day): `swift test` green in **Units** (52 tests: PR #1's 40 plus slice 1's four-to-six, four-to-one, awkward-scale and kitchen-conversion cases ported to the `scaled → displayed → friendlyRounded → formatted` pipeline), **Capture** 12 and **RecipeModel** 9; `xcodebuild build` for the iPhone 17 Pro simulator succeeded. On the simulator: a recipe with "1/4 cup butter", "2 tsp salt", "9 tbsp sugar", "1 cup milk" for 4 read as written, scaled to 6 as 6 tbsp, 1 tbsp, 13½ tbsp, 1½ cups; Metric at 6 gave 90 mL, 1 tbsp (spoon unchanged), 200 mL, 350 mL; Metric at 4 gave 60 mL, 2 tsp, 135 mL, 235 mL; US at 4 kept 9 tbsp as 9 tbsp.
- Fixed during that run: the More menu nesting (above); the large title disappearing when a filter emptied the list (the grid/list now stays in place with the message as an overlay); 9 tbsp rounding to a half cup that was 11% short (the original unit is now always a fallback candidate); the Cook button hidden behind the tab bar; number fields that only focused when the text itself was tapped.

### Known limitations to revisit

- Ranges keep only the low end ("2-3 cloves" scales from 2); the raw text keeps the rest. Showing a scaled range needs an upper-bound field on `Ingredient` (a schema change, so not done here).
- No volume ↔ mass conversion (cups of flour to grams) until ingredient densities exist; flour in metric shows milliliters.
- Count items aren't pluralized ("1 1/2 large egg"), and sizes stay in the item text ("large eggs").
- Title, course and photo edits are not versioned (see decisions above).
