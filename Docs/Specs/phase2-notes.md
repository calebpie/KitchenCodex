### Decisions made in this slice

- **Needs a Look stays in the Filter list.** The brief's and 04's kinds are Course, Cooked, Favorites, Collection, Cookbook and Tag; Needs a Look was a Filter entry since row 9 and `RecipeFilter` keeps it, so it is a switch beside Favorites rather than a filter that could be set (and shown as a token) with no row to unset it.
- **Tokens follow the rule literally.** "Kind: selection" for every list kind, so Cooked reads "Cooked: Never Cooked" and "Cooked: Cooked"; the switches read their name alone. The second reads oddly (row 46).
- **One value for Collection, Cookbook and Tag.** `RecipeFilter` holds one of each and the brief keeps the model, so "several allowed where the kind allows it" means Course only; a second tap on the chosen value clears it. Unset kinds read "Any".
- **One presentation for both widths.** `.popover` on the Filter button is the iPad popover and adapts to a sheet in compact width, so the menu bar's Filter… and the toolbar open the same thing, anchored to the button on iPad.
- **Add (+) on Cookbooks is a one-item menu**, naming the way in as the Recipes Add does; Scan Sources Page joins it in slice 7 (row 51 asks whether a plain button is better until then).
- **The subtitle is split off.** Open Library often folds it into the title ("The Food Lab: Better Home Cooking Through Science"); the first ": " splits it, the card shows the subtitle, and the shelf keeps the short title (`Book` has no subtitle field).
- **A search result's ISBN is its best-matching edition's** (`editions.isbn`, ISBN-13 first), and its year is the work's first publication year; the work's own `isbn` list mixes every edition, foreign ones first.
- **Details by hand replace the lookup in place** rather than pushing, so Cancel stays in the corner; they start from what was typed.
- **A book already on the shelf by ISBN opens instead of a duplicate** ("On Shelf").
- **Covers**: the Open Library "L" image, kept only if it is at least 1 KB, re-encoded to the 900 px, 0.8 JPEG of a recipe's cover copy; Edit Book Info takes a Photos image the same way.
- **The punchlist lists render when entries exist** (read-only; "Scan now" waits for slice 5), so the control does something the day slice 5 fills it.
- **The scanner checks the description first**, then `DataScannerViewController.isSupported` and `isAvailable`, so nothing touches the camera without one. Caleb added the description during the slice (`c2c96b3`); the simulator still hides the button (it can't scan).
- **Google Books' key** would come from `GoogleBooksAPIKey` in the Info dictionary; a custom key needs an Info.plist entry or build setting, Caleb's to add.
- **Escape stays `.keyboardShortcut(.cancelAction)`.** A UIKit first-responder view answering Escape was tried and dropped: ⌘K on the same toolbar button closed the sheet, so sheets' shortcuts are reachable, and the simulator never delivered Escape to anything (below).

### Schema additions, and why

- **`Reference`, `ReferenceSection`** as 02 §4.2 has them. `tags` is `[String]`, as 02's `tags[]` and `Book.reviewedLayouts`; the section's order is `sortOrder`, as every ordered model here. The source is required in use and optional in the schema (CloudKit).
- **`SourceInfo.firstPage`, `lastPage`**: the reference's "book + pageRange", and the pages of a recipe stitched over a spread. **`SourceInfo.captureMethod`** (`CaptureMethod` manual · web · library · scan · photo): 04 §5.1 "Which book" has the source say how a recipe came in. **`SourceInfo.reviewedAt`**: nil on a scan saved straight to the library, which slice 3's provenance calls "Accepted automatically".
- **`RecipeImage.regions`** (`Data`, `ScanRegions` JSON: format version, layout signature, page number, and per block kind, role, rect in unit page coordinates, text, confidence and the item it became), **`RecipeImage.pageKind`** (`ScanPageKind`; nil while a captured page waits in slice 5's queue) and **`RecipeImage.book`** (`Book.pageScans`, nullify, as `Book.recipes`): the book's contents, index and Sources scans and the session's unprocessed captures have somewhere to live.
- **`Recipe.extractionConfidence`**: the page reading's combined figure, kept on the recipe across edits; a version's `captureConfidence` stays the import's.
- **`RecipeVersion.reviewReasons`** (`ReviewReason` raw values): slice 5's background queue reviews later than it reads, and the review screen and Needs a Look can say why.
- **`BookEntry.reference`**: contents pages list essays and technique chapters beside recipes (two of the fixture books do); the punchlist can count them captured once slice 7 saves them as references.
- **Store check**: both simulators' stores opened in place on the new schema (below). No rule was bent.

### Decisions made in this slice

- **Deterministic first, and enough on its own here.** After stitching, no fixture recipe lacked a title, two ingredients or a step, so the model refinement never ran on the set; the comparison runs below say why that is the right default.
- **Steps are printed paragraphs.** The recognizer's paragraphs are a hint, not the answer: it splits printed paragraphs (Bouchon) and sometimes joins two (Gjelina). Steps follow indents, spacing, numbers and run-in heads, measured against fitted column edges so a tilted photo doesn't move them.
- **Several recipes on a photo read as several recipes** (rows 60, 61). A component recipe over several pages stays one recipe with groups.
- **A line with no amount reads cleanly** for the scan review gate; only a line whose amount went unread asks for review (row 58).
- **The layout signature** is the title's setting (letterspaced capitals centred or flush left, `wc`/`wl`, or set larger, `s`), where the ingredients sit against the method and in how many columns, and whether the method is numbered and in how many columns: `t:wc i:above-n m:para-2` for Bouchon, `t:wl i:above-n m:para-1` Gjelina, `t:s i:left-1 m:para-1` Plenty, `t:s i:left-1 m:num-1` Nobu. Alignment is left out of `s` titles: a long title fills the measure either way.
- **Folios on contents pages** count only with a separator; a bare number there is as likely an entry's page.
- **`ImportMethod` unchanged**: scans are `.pageText`, `.model` once refined, so the app's exhaustive switches need no edit.

### Decisions made in this slice

- **The review screen is one view for a fresh scan and a stored one.** Its model is built from a `PageReading` or from a recipe's page scans (`ScanRegions` and the current version, matched by each region's item), so Review Scan in More reviews what was saved.
- **A reassigned region feeds one thing.** Every item it fed goes and it becomes the new part in reading order; a step broken over two regions goes whole when either is reassigned, and the other can be assigned back. Simpler to predict than partial edits, and Undo is always there.
- **Edits are in place**: the first tap on an item shows its source, the second edits its wording; no separate form.
- **Gating per reading.** Readings are taken in capture order; one is reviewed when any recipe on it is the book's first or of a layout the book hasn't confirmed, so a page's two recipes are reviewed together, and a later page of the layout confirmed by an earlier review in the same scan saves straight. A page from no book is always reviewed (no layout to confirm), and so is a recipe with no title.
- **Low confidence saves straight with a look asked for**, as the brief and 03 §3.6 have it: Needs a Look and the review row, not the review screen.
- **The page is stored upright**, so the regions' rects are stored as read. Two recipes on one photo each get their own `RecipeImage` with the same JPEG (one encoding) and their own regions: a `RecipeImage` belongs to one recipe.
- **Regions JSON**: `kind` is Capture's raw value ("listItem", where `ScanRegion`'s comment says "list"); a printed photo chosen as the cover has role "cover", an app-level value beside Capture's roles; an unchosen photo has no role; the text is the printed lines joined by newlines, so review can split them again.
- **A first photo that opens mid-recipe** continues a page that wasn't scanned: shown in review, skipped unless included, never named by the model.
- **Which book? lists the shelf by last use** (the latest recipe saved from it, else when it was added).
- **No Cookbook picker in the editor and no Add to Cookbook in Select mode** (row 49): the book comes only from a scan.
- **The Simulator reads a 4,096-pixel copy** (`#if targetEnvironment(simulator)`): its Vision returns line fragments at the wrong places on a full 24-megapixel photo (below). Devices and the harness read at full size.
- **Rescan** reads the same photos again (a stored review reads the stored pages) and drops the review's changes after asking.
- **Commit trailer**: `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5`, the model that did the work.

### Decisions made in this slice

- **A custom AVFoundation camera, not VisionKit's.** The decided controls (the pill, the Recipe · Photo switch, Add Next Page, the stack) can't sit on `VNDocumentCameraViewController`; phase2-plan's slice 4 text is updated.
- **The camera opens with Scan Page on a device** (Flow B step 2), From Photo and Files a Close away.
- **Add Next Page captures the next page as soon as it is held still**, so turning pages is one tap each; the shutter captures at once. Done sits at the top right, since 03 §3.5 gives the shutter's right to Add Next Page. Auto-capture is off by default and remembered (row 79 lists these choices for Caleb).
- **The live pill is the gate on preview frames** (a 1,600-pixel frame once a second, off the main actor, both measures at the thresholds tuned on full photos); a capture is measured at full size before cleanup and that reading goes with it (sharpening would flatter a soft photo). Cost: one gate pass a second.
- **The lens prompt's "low confidence"** is the page's mean OCR confidence under the structurer's 0.45 mark; the four hazy Bouchon photos (0.64–0.65) would not trip it.
- **Cleanup crops only to a believable page.** On the fixtures Vision's quads for a page filling the frame run along the frame's edges with heavy skew, and cropping to them changed 12 of 65 readings (folios lost); a page is cropped to only when convex, clear of the frame's edges and with opposite sides alike, else the frame is cleaned whole. Levels and sharpening as briefed (numbers below).
- **The original capture is dropped**: it is never written; the cleaned page is read and stored (row 76 asks whether Caleb wants the original kept; phase2-plan said "the original kept").
- **Captures are read as they are taken**, so the iPad preview and the lens prompt have readings, and Done has little left; stitching and the model wait for Done.
- **Recipe · Photo is sticky within a camera session**, starting at Recipe each time the camera opens; a Photo capture's cover candidate becomes the cover only when the recipe has none (slice 8's cover choice picks among them).
- **A barcode photo is looked for only among pages that read as no recipe**, so recipe pages cost nothing; every barcode photo leaves the scan, the first ISBN counts.
- **Recipes saved straight take the book's last course too** (row 78 asks): a book is scanned chapter by chapter, and row 73's complaint was scans landing in No Course.
- **Read Fixtures reads picked photos, not the album by name, and shares its report**: the app has no `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`, which PhotoKit needs to open an album, and its Documents folder isn't shown in Files (no `UIFileSharingEnabled`); both are Info.plist keys, outside a slice (row 75). It reads without the model, as the harness does, with the app's reader settings.
- **The sync flag is only on the whole-library view** and opens the detail as a sheet; Profile's row pushes the same detail.
- **Commit trailer**: `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5`, the model that did the work.

### Decisions made in this slice

- **Deterministic first, the model rarely.** No fixture contents page needed the model (each read 30 or more entries, or read as an overview of chapters); `structureContents` exists for a page that doesn't, and its reading must beat the deterministic one on paged entries in a rising order.
- **An overview page is chapters.** Nobu's first contents page and both of Bouchon's list chapters with first pages and no recipes; they become sections with a first page, which the detailed pages fill (Nobu) or which stay empty (Bouchon, whose list then grows as recipes are scanned, each gaining an entry in the chapter its page falls in).
- **Index pages fill in only where the contents listed no recipes**: an index lists ingredients and recipes alike, and the contents' list is cleaner.
- **Everything saves straight; the review queue is the book's recipes that want a look.** Recipes are saved as they are read, so Close loses nothing and the list keeps up; the queue holds the first recipe of each layout (the forced reasons), a capture the cook flagged, and a reading with low-confidence reasons, as 02 Flow C step 7 has it. A layout already seen in the session isn't queued again.
- **The queue is paged, not swiped**: Previous, Skip and Save & Next, since a horizontal swipe would fight review's page drags and the lasso (row 94 asks).
- **Courses live in the session's state and on the recipes.** No schema change is available, so a chapter's chosen course is kept in the book's `session.json`; with nothing chosen, a chapter takes the course its saved recipes carry most, else its title's default. It replaces the book's-last-course default whenever the book has sections (one-off scans included).
- **A recipe not on the list gains an entry** in the chapter its page falls in, in the session only, so the counts stay true; a page scanned on its own only links to entries that exist.
- **A page shot again saves a copy** and leaves the list where it is, the captures sheet saying so; the cook deletes the copy there (row 93 asks).
- **A capture with no page number is the item current when it was taken** (the camera); photos chosen in a batch follow the list as each is read.
- **A recipe-mode capture that reads as a photo plate is a photo**, attached like a Photo capture; Nobu's plates went to the recipe before them.
- **Counts are per sitting**: the strip counts this sitting's captures (and any left waiting); finished captures from earlier sittings and their files are let go when a sitting opens.
- **No `BGProcessingTask`**: the queue persists and resumes at launch; registering a background task needs the "processing" background mode and `BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers` (row 92 asks).
- **Add Cookbook leaves the menu** (04 §5.1 "Add Cookbook": it retires into Scan Cookbook), so the menus are identical with Scan Cookbook on both (row 90's aim); the plan's rider said "Add Cookbook on the Recipes view's Add menu too", written before the retirement.
- **The book session's Close pauses without asking to discard**; the camera's own Close is hidden there.
